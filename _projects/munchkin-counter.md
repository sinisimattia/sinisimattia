Munchkin Counter is a little companion app for the card game Munchkin. Instead of
juggling dice and scraps of paper, each player gets a clean counter for their level
and combat bonuses, so the table can focus on the backstabbing.

## Highlights

- **Per-player tracking** — level and bonus counters for everyone at the table.
- **Tap to adjust** — quick increment and decrement, no fiddly inputs.
- **Built for fun** — a small weekend tool that makes game night smoother.
