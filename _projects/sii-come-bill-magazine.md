Sii Come Bill Magazine is an online publication built around the tagline
*"l'intelligenza che fa moda"* — covering culture, style, and ideas with a sharp,
minimalist voice. The reading experience is fast and uncluttered, keeping the writing
and imagery front and centre.

I worked on it as both **designer and WordPress developer**, owning the project end to end:

- **Design** — defined the layout, typography, and overall reading experience.
- **WordPress development** — built out the theme and templates to bring the design to life.
- **Editor-friendly** — set up the site so the team can publish and manage content without touching code.
