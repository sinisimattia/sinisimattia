VOKU is an event management app I'm currently in the early stages of planning. The idea
is a single tool that carries an event through its whole life cycle — from setting it up
and managing attendees to running it smoothly on the day.

It's still on the drawing board: the concept and groundwork are taking shape and there's
nothing public to try just yet, but here's where it's headed.

## What's planned

- **Event setup** — create events, schedules, and the details that go with them.
- **Attendee management** — handle guests, registrations, and the people side of an event.
- **On-the-day tools** — keep things running once the event is actually live.

Follow along on [GitHub](https://github.com/voku-app) as it takes shape.
