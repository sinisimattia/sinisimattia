Jongo is an Italian construction company — *la tua prossima azienda edile* — handling
building work, renovations, and everything that comes with them. The site gives them a
clean, modern presence to show what they do and make it easy for clients to get in touch.

I worked on it as both **designer and developer**, owning the project end to end:

- **Design** — defined the layout, typography, and overall feel of the site.
- **Development** — built it as a fast, static website that's quick to load and simple to maintain.
- **Lead generation** — set up clear calls to action so visitors can reach out and request work.
