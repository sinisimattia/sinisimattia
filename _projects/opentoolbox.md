The Open Toolbox is an open-source initiative behind a set of free, community-minded
websites that gather useful resources — guides, tutorials, frameworks, and tools —
and organise them so they're easy to browse in one place. The idea is simple: instead
of bookmarking the same links over and over, collect the good stuff openly so everyone
can benefit.

## Highlights

- **Curated resources** — handpicked guides, tutorials, and tools grouped by topic.
- **Free and open** — the sites and their content are open for anyone to use and contribute to.
- **Built to grow** — designed as a home for multiple themed toolboxes over time.

It's open to contributions.
