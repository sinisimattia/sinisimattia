BillOver 3.0 is a creator collective founded by Andrea Nuzzo and Michele Casula —
professionals and "Unfluencers" who bring experience and heart to communication, out
to make a difference rather than just chase numbers. Their site is the hub for who they
are and the work they do.

I worked on it as both **designer and WordPress developer**, owning the project end to end:

- **Design** — defined the layout, typography, and overall feel of the site.
- **WordPress development** — built out the theme and templates to bring the design to life.
- **Editor-friendly** — set up the site so the team can manage content without touching code.
