OSTIACORE is the home of the hardcore music scene from the Litorale Romano — a hub for
upcoming gigs, the bands behind them, and everything happening in the local scene. The
site keeps the community in the loop and gives the scene a place to point people to.

I worked on it as both **designer and developer**, owning the project end to end:

- **Design** — defined the layout, typography, and overall feel of the site.
- **Development** — built it as a fast, static site with an easy way to keep events up to date.
- **Events-first** — structured everything around upcoming shows so the next gig is always front and centre.
