Agenxia is a wine representation agency founded in 2009 that brings Italian and
international wineries to restaurants, wine bars and shops across Northern Italy:
Milan, Monza Brianza, Lodi, Varese, Como and Lecco. Its portfolio counts more than
2,000 labels, from whites, reds and rosés to sparkling and dessert wines, spirits,
craft beers and specialty foods, served to over a thousand clients.

The website is the agency's showcase: it introduces the team, presents the brands
it represents, and keeps clients up to date through the blog and events pages, with
a direct line to get in touch.
