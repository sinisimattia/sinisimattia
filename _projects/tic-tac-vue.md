Tic-Tac-Vue is exactly what it sounds like: classic tic-tac-toe, rebuilt as a small
Vue experiment. It started as an excuse to play with reactivity and ended up being a
neat little game you can actually share with friends.

## Highlights

- **Instant play** — open the page and start a round, no setup.
- **Reactive board** — every move updates the state and win-detection on the fly.
- **Just for fun** — small, self-contained, and a good playground for Vue ideas.
